About the Machine Learning Predictor

This decision support tool uses a Random Forest Classifier trained on clinical patient cohorts with SMOTE oversampling to assess risk factors including age, hyperglycemia, cardiovascular history, and BMI.

  • Top Predictive Factors: Age (~41.7%), Average Glucose Level (~18.0%), and BMI (~14.1%).
  • Intended Use: Educational screening and preliminary clinical triage. Not a replacement for formal neurological diagnosis.
